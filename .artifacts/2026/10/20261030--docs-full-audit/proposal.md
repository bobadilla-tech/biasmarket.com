## 2. Summary

Counts are over the 109 numbered findings in section 4 (computed by the commands
in section 8, after round-1 revisions): by type 30 error, 32 stale, 32 gap, 10
dup, 4 aspirational, 1 orphan; by severity 11 blocking, 61 important, 37 minor.
Of the 109, the lead session re-verified those marked `V`; the rest are
agent-reported (`A`) and were re-checked in part by the round-1 accuracy review.

Top problems:

1. **Buyer proof upload is built, but six docs say it is not** (`product.md`,
   `architecture.md`, `security-payments.md` §10, `buyer-flow.md`,
   `seller-flow.md`, `roadmap.md`). `OrderPayment.source = BUYER_SUBMITTED`,
   `buyer-payment-proof.service.ts`, and `POST /public/orders/:token/payments`
   exist.
2. **Whole shipped subsystems have no doc**: assisted orders with private links,
   global buyer accounts and Google claim, order events, identity
   retention/erasure (90 days after close), audit-log actions, coupons/premium,
   workers jobs, rate-limit inventory.
3. **Security claims that are false**: passwords are said to use `bcrypt` (code
   uses `better-auth/crypto`), ownership is said to live at the DTO layer (it
   lives in `assertOwnership` in the service layer), `admin.md` says there is no
   client-side admin redirect (the admin layout redirects).
4. **Navigation is broken**: no `docs/readme.md`; `docs/core/readme.md` holds
   the Docker dev guide instead of an index; ten broken anchors; `docker.md` is
   a dated audit narrative; `blue-green-migrations.md` (674 lines) mixes
   runbook, concept, provisioning, and history.
5. **Ops gaps**: no restore runbook or statement that there is no scheduled
   backup, Terraform documented only in `.artifacts/guides/`, no env-var
   reference, Cloudflare/edge setup undocumented, `ci.md` omits half the CI
   jobs.
